Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy describes how Crate handles information when you organize record artwork, create posters, and use collector conversations.

Information stored on your device

Crate stores your chosen cover photos, music titles, artist names, record details, shelf selections, poster text and images, guest nickname, messages, reports, and blocked or dismissed profiles on your device. No account registration is required. Collector profiles are curated examples included with the app. Conversations do not receive replies, and messages are not sent to another person.

Photos, camera, and voice

Crate requests your permission when you choose a photo, take a cover photo, or use voice input. You can decline permission and continue with available alternatives. Camera access is used to capture cover images. Microphone access is used only for requested voice input, not to record music. Where speech recognition is available, processing is subject to the device's system speech service and its authorization settings.

Sharing and third-party services

Crate does not upload your collection, poster text, or conversations to an application backend. You choose whether to share an image using the system share sheet. Only share material when you agree to send it to the selected destination; that third-party destination handles it under its own policy. Opening these legal pages connects to their hosting provider, which may process normal web request data such as IP address and browser information.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. Crate stores transaction identifiers and the remaining purchased sleeves balance to deliver purchases once and track poster creation. Crate does not receive your payment card details. Sleeves are consumed when a poster is successfully composed.

Notifications

Daily reminders require notification permission. You can change notification permissions in device settings and disable reminders in Crate.

Retention and controls

You can edit or delete records and remove their associated posters. Clear Crate removes collection and conversation information while retaining the purchased balance and transaction history needed to prevent duplicate delivery. Uninstalling the application may remove information stored on your device. Device backups may be governed by your system settings. Do not rely on Crate as the only copy of important artwork.

Safety

On-device content checks help prevent inappropriate text from being saved or sent within the application. Reports are saved on the device and are not automatically delivered to a moderation server. Blocking removes a profile from recommendations and conversations.

Contact and changes

Contact: crate.archive.contact@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when product behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
